# Plastics Desk: how the figures work

The six main sections are Dashboard, Molds, Production, Products & Inventory, Sales & Invoices, and Finances. Decisions, calculators, documents, and reports remain accessible from the top bar. The historical accounting and decision records remain stored; the manufacturing screens add records rather than replacing old rows. Test and live workspaces remain separate.

The production estimate is `units × grams per unit × SAR per gram + setup + packaging + transport + other charges`. A supplier quote may override the contracted total while the calculated estimate stays visible. A final cost can be entered before the first receipt. Accepted units enter inventory at the contracted or final cost divided by ordered units. Rejected and damaged receipts never become sellable stock. Cost of goods sold uses the weighted average inventory value at each dispatch. Returns restore their original dispatch cost. Write-offs reduce stock and estimated profit. Stock adjustments require an operator, a reason, and a unique reference.

Invoices create sales revenue excluding entered tax when issued. The gross invoice total, including tax, establishes the customer balance. Dispatch reduces inventory and records cost of goods sold. Collections and refunds affect the single cash register. Credits reduce invoice revenue and the customer balance; receiving returned goods is a separate action. The generated invoice PDF is an **internal sales document**, not a verified ZATCA tax invoice. Partial collections and supplier installments appear once as `FinanceTransaction` rows. Mold payments are asset purchases, production payments settle production obligations, capital contributions are funding, and funder repayments are liability payments. None of those are included again as operating expenses. Historical posted journals are displayed separately because the same event may exist in both systems.

Opening cash and bank balances must be verified. Named accounts calculate from their opening balance plus later assigned payments. A payment with no named account still appears in total money in/out but cannot be assigned to a specific bank balance automatically. The dashboard's indicative cash after obligations subtracts recorded mold, production, and confirmed repayable funding balances from known account cash. It does not include unrecorded tax, reserves, or commitments and is not an approval to distribute money.

External contributions are either **undetermined**, **repayable**, or **equity** as entered by an operator. There is no default contributor and no assumption that an individual funded the business. Retained profit and reinvestment budgets are planning allocations, not new cash inflows. The repayment and 50/50 distribution calculators are plans only; they do not send payments or create legal obligations. The existing two-person decision flow retains individual votes, comments, supporting documents, and its audit trail.

Before live financial use, obtain the accountant's confirmation of the authoritative accounting system and opening balances; legal confirmation of any repayable funding and the partners' distribution agreement; a policy for tax, reserves, rejects, supplier credits, and stock valuation; and a malware-scanning service for live document access. The current file security deliberately quarantines new live documents until scanning is configured. Do not treat internal PDFs as statutory Saudi tax invoices.
