# Stages and demonstrations

Use only the isolated Test workspace for the following. Real facts remain in the live workspace as unverified drafts. The automated Chromium demonstration repeats the operating sequence through the actual web forms with two independent accounts.

## Stage 1 — controls and ledger

1. Start the services using README.md. Seed both workspaces; observe no live users or posted transactions.
2. Issue two separate **test-workspace** invitations and enroll each test operator with an authenticator. No fabricated real email addresses or built-in passwords are seeded.
3. Upload a text file explicitly labeled synthetic. Open **Decisions & approvals → New decision → funding_direct**. Supply the private evidence UUID and `classification=loan`, a unique reference, an amount of `10000`, explicit date/VAT basis/conditions and a test funder identity.
4. Submit, reauthenticate, and sign as the first operator. Attempt execution: it is unavailable/rejected. Log in independently as the second operator and sign the same version. Record test execution.
5. The journal debits supplier advance 10,000 and credits funding loan 10,000. Bank remains unchanged. Revise an unexecuted decision: its old signatures remain visible while the new version has none.

Evidence: `tests/test_finance.py`, `tests/test_approvals.py`, `tests/test_security.py`, funding and journal tests in `tests/test_workflow.py`.

## Stage 2 — a full synthetic batch

The browser test creates a 100,000-unit purchase, releases production against it, records 100,000 pieces at SAR 36,900, accepts quality, recognizes the supplier bill, reserves a 1,000-piece sale at SAR 2 net with 0% VAT **for this synthetic case**, delivers it, records a management invoice and collects SAR 1,000. It then imports and matches the bank row.

Expected posted result for that case:

- Inventory remaining: 99,000 pieces costing SAR 36,531.
- Revenue: SAR 2,000; COGS: SAR 369; gross profit: SAR 1,631.
- Customer outstanding: SAR 1,000; bank cash movement: +SAR 1,000.
- Supplier payable: SAR 36,900. Statement row matched once.
- Nine decisions, eighteen distinct-version signatures, nine executions. Management invoice adds no duplicate revenue or COGS.
- Opening cash remains unknown; +1,000 movement is not presented as a verified bank balance.

Additional service tests cover partial billing/payment, rejection, returns and credits/refunds, quarantined stock, weighted-average costing, duplicate imports and concurrent reservation. Screenshots are generated by the real-browser test in `docs/screenshots/`.

## Stage 3 — documents and recoverability

1. Open any of the eight templates. Missing fields remain explicit and the PDF is watermarked by prominent draft text. It is not a complete legal agreement.
2. An internal contract-approval decision binds the exact template fields and their digest. Editing a submitted draft changes the hash and blocks execution until both operators approve a fresh decision version.
3. Attach uploaded signature evidence to an internally approved contract: status remains `uploaded_evidence_unverified`, never certified or externally executed.
4. Export a tenant archive with records, original documents, SHA-256 manifest, approval evidence, audit and access metadata. The user-facing archive intentionally omits passwords/MFA secrets.
5. Full disaster recovery uses `scripts/backup.py` for a consistent PostgreSQL archive, including private documents and authentication configuration. The automated restore test restores into an empty database and verifies all these elements.

Evidence: `tests/test_restore.py`, contract/export/security tests. External provider and statutory tax-invoice sandbox validation are blocked until selection, requirements and credentials are available; there is no fake success response.

Full Arabic translation and fonts/PDF shaping, scheduled reminders, advanced forecasts, product analytics, and external integrations can be reviewed separately. No messages are sent without an explicit authorized workflow.
