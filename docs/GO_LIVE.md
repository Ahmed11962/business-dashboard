# Information needed before live business use

The current application is for private management testing. No external payment, bank transfer, statutory invoice or tax filing is connected. The Test workspace stays separate from the live workspace. Live financial execution remains disabled.

| Needed from the operators or advisers | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Sami's invitation email and each operator's verified authority | Sami must enroll independently; neither operator may approve for the other. |
| Accountant's confirmation of the authoritative accounting system and opening balances | The new management register is not automatically the legal entity's books. A current bank/cash balance requires a dated verified opening balance. |
| VAT status, invoice requirements, and category/treatment policy | Estimated income and expenses need professional review before statutory reporting. |
| Hosting, malware scanning, backups and recovery owner | Live uploaded files remain quarantined until scanning is configured. A retained backup and restore process must be agreed. |
| Approved operating policy for one- versus two-person decisions | Proposal thresholds are configurable per decision; the business should define which decisions require both people. |

The app never assumes who paid, who owns an asset, or how a transaction was funded. Payer/payee is optional on each new transaction. Any older record with different assumptions is preserved as historical evidence and should be reviewed with the accountant rather than silently changed.
